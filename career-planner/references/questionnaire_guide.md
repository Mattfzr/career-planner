# Career Assessment Questionnaire Guide

## Comprehensive Question Set

Use these questions to gather complete background information from users. Adapt based on user responses and career stage.

### Personal Information
- Full name
- Current location
- Contact information (email, phone)
- LinkedIn profile URL (optional)
- Portfolio/website (optional)

### Education Background
- Highest degree earned
- Field of study/major
- Institution name and location
- Graduation year
- GPA (optional)
- Relevant coursework
- Academic honors/awards
- Study abroad experiences
- Certifications or additional training

### Work Experience
**For each position:**
- Job title
- Company/organization name
- Employment dates (month/year)
- Location (city, country)
- Employment type (full-time, part-time, internship, contract)
- Key responsibilities
- Major achievements (quantify when possible)
- Skills utilized
- Reason for leaving (optional)

### Skills Assessment
**Technical Skills:**
- Programming languages and proficiency levels
- Software/tools/platforms
- Frameworks and libraries
- Database systems
- Cloud platforms
- Design tools
- Laboratory techniques (for science fields)
- Equipment operation

**Soft Skills:**
- Communication (written, verbal, presentation)
- Leadership and management
- Problem-solving
- Teamwork and collaboration
- Time management
- Adaptability
- Creativity
- Critical thinking

**Language Proficiency:**
- Languages spoken and proficiency levels (native, fluent, intermediate, basic)

### Career Preferences
**Work Environment:**
- Office setting preference (remote, hybrid, on-site)
- Company size preference (startup, SME, large corporation)
- Industry preferences (tech, finance, healthcare, education, etc.)
- Company culture preferences (fast-paced, structured, collaborative, etc.)

**Compensation Expectations:**
- Desired salary range (annual or hourly)
- Bonus/commission expectations
- Equity/stock options interest
- Benefits importance (health insurance, retirement, etc.)

**Work-Life Balance:**
- Preferred working hours
- Willingness to travel
- Overtime expectations
- Vacation/time-off preferences

**Location Preferences:**
- Geographic preferences (specific cities, countries, regions)
- Relocation willingness
- Remote work timezone flexibility

### Career Goals
**Short-term (1-2 years):**
- Desired job title/role
- Skills to develop
- Industries to enter
- Target companies

**Long-term (3-5 years):**
- Career advancement goals
- Leadership aspirations
- Industry specialization
- Work-life integration goals

**Ultimate Career Vision:**
- Dream job/role
- Impact goals
- Legacy aspirations

### Additional Information
**Projects and Portfolio:**
- Significant academic projects
- Personal projects
- Open source contributions
- Volunteer work
- Publications/presentations

**Professional Development:**
- Conferences attended
- Workshops completed
- Professional memberships
- Mentoring experience

**Personal Motivators:**
- What excites you about work?
- What work environments drain your energy?
- Values most important in a workplace
- Deal-breakers in a job

## Follow-up Questions by Category

### For Recent Graduates:
- What aspects of your degree program were most engaging?
- Which projects or courses sparked your interest?
- What internship experiences were most valuable?
- How do you envision applying your academic knowledge?

### For Career Changers:
- What motivated your career transition?
- What transferable skills do you bring from previous roles?
- What new skills have you developed for this transition?
- What challenges do you anticipate?

### For Experienced Professionals:
- What leadership experiences have you had?
- What industry trends are you following?
- What mentoring or training have you provided?
- What strategic contributions have you made?

### For Specific Industries:

**Tech/Engineering:**
- Preferred tech stack
- Development methodologies experience
- Project lifecycle involvement
- Code repository contributions

**Business/Finance:**
- Analytical tools proficiency
- Financial modeling experience
- Client management experience
- Regulatory knowledge

**Creative Fields:**
- Portfolio highlights
- Creative process description
- Software/tools proficiency
- Client/collaborator feedback examples

## Assessment Tips

1. **Build Rapport**: Start with less personal questions, progress to deeper topics
2. **Active Listening**: Note hesitations or enthusiasm in responses
3. **Clarify Ambiguities**: Ask for specific examples when responses are vague
4. **Connect Dots**: Identify patterns between education, experience, and preferences
5. **Validate Understanding**: Summarize key points back to the user
6. **Identify Gaps**: Note missing information that would strengthen the profile
7. **Prioritize**: Focus on most relevant information for target roles

## Common Red Flags to Explore

- **Vague responses**: "I did various tasks" → Ask for specific responsibilities
- **Gaps in employment**: Explore reasons and activities during gaps
- **Overqualification**: Discuss willingness to accept junior roles
- **Underqualification**: Identify skill development opportunities
- **Conflicting preferences**: Remote work desire with industries requiring on-site presence
